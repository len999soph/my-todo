# Sophia's Social Studies Study Hub 📚

Hi Sophia! This folder is your social studies study space for **8th grade** at
John Jay Middle School (and it'll grow with you into high school).

## How to use the tutor agent

You have a personal tutor agent set up. To use it, just ask Claude things like:

- **"Use my social-studies-tutor to teach me about Reconstruction."**
- **"Quiz me on the Progressive Era."**
- **"I read article 01, quiz me on it."**
- **"Help me review for a test on industrialization."**
- **"Explain the New Deal like I'm confused."**
- **"Go deeper on World War I causes."**

The tutor is patient, explains the *why* behind history (not just facts to
memorize), and quizzes you the way a real NY State test would.

## The study loop (how to actually get better)

1. **Read** an article (start with `articles/01-reconstruction.md`).
2. **Come back** and tell the tutor "I finished article 01, quiz me."
3. **Answer** the questions — getting some wrong is normal and good!
4. **Review** the ones you missed with the tutor until they click.
5. **Repeat** with the next unit.

You've already got a 94 — this is about turning a "good" into "I've got this,"
and getting a head start on the harder years. You've got this. 💪
